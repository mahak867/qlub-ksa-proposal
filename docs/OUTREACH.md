## If they reply

- **"Tell me more"**: offer a 15-minute call and send the brief. Prepare three questions about their KSA priorities; listen more than you present.
- **"We already do this"**: thank them, ask what you got wrong, and update the brief. That exchange is still a relationship.
- **"Are you looking for a role?"**: say yes plainly, name the role you applied for, and say what you'd want to work on first.

## Tone rules

- "Users report", never "Qlub does". You have reviews, not proof.
- No words like scam, illegal, broken or dark pattern, even when quoting.
- Don't post about this publicly or tag Qlub before someone there has seen it.
- Never mention anything you couldn't see as an ordinary user. Don't test or probe their live systems.
