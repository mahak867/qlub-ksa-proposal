# Demo script (90 seconds)

Record on a phone-sized browser window, in Arabic, switching to English once. Before recording: open **Demo controls**, press **Reset demo data**, and keep that page open in a second tab. Speak slowly; the words below fit 90 seconds at a calm pace.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Home page, then press **Scan table 12** | "I read a hundred low-star reviews of pay-at-table apps in Saudi Arabia. People like paying by QR. What frustrates them is everything around it. This is a concept that fixes four things." |
| 0:10–0:22 | Bill page: point at the "No app needed" banner. Press **Pay the bill**, choose **Split equally**, **Continue** | "One: no app, no sign-up. Scan, and split the bill by item, equally, or by amount." |
| 0:22–0:38 | Review page: point at the tip row (No tip selected), then the large total above the pay button. Press **Pay with mada** | "Two: the tip is optional and nothing is pre-selected. The full total is above the pay button, and the server refuses to charge anything different." |
| 0:38–0:50 | Paid screen, then **View receipt**. Scroll to the optional offer, press **No thanks** | "Three: paying never signs you up. The membership offer comes after payment, unticked, with the price and how to cancel in one sentence." |
| 0:50–1:08 | Second tab: set gateway to **Hang**. First tab: pay the second share; "Checking with your bank" appears. Second tab: **Run recovery now**. First tab shows "You were not charged". Set gateway to **Approve**, press **Try again**, **Continue**, pay | "Four: no stuck payments. Here the bank doesn't answer. Within ninety seconds the diner sees 'not charged' and can retry safely. It can never charge twice." |
| 1:08–1:20 | Receipt: the tax invoice with its QR code. Toggle to English | "Two people paid, and the restaurant gets one tax invoice with a ZATCA QR code. Everything works in Arabic and English." |
| 1:20–1:30 | Restaurant view, then the brief | "The restaurant sees every payment reconciled. It's an independent concept, built with AI agents, with thirty-nine tests. I'd value fifteen minutes of your feedback." |

Notes:
- Say "concept" and "independent" on camera. Don't show any Qlub logo or screenshot.
- If you want an Arabic voice-over, record the screen once and narrate separately.
- Keep the video unlisted until someone at Qlub has seen it.
